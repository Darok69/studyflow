The four ECSC organs (1952) and their heirs today

High Authority ➜ Commission

interests of the community; independent persons, "supranational character"; decisions directly binding; first president Monnet

Council of Ministers ➜ Council of the EU

interests of the member states; ministers of national governments

Common Assembly ➜ European Parliament

interests of the people; directly elected since 1980; strengthened by SEA

Court of Justice ➜ ECJ

legal control; seat in Luxembourg; obligatory dispute settlement

1957: the three Communities share Parliament and Court from the start, Commission and Council only from 1967 (Merger Treaty 1965) — separate legal communities, gradually merging organs.

What makes an organisation supranational — four marks (the exam answer)

1

Majority voting

with power to bind outvoted member states

2

Obligatory dispute settlement

a compulsory court — the ECJ

3

Independent institutions

Art. 9 ECSC: states must not influence the High Authority

4

Direct effect + primacy

applies inside national law without transformation and prevails

Ordinary international law binds only consenting states, through their own organs; supranational law binds them against their vote and reaches into their courts directly.

"Each member state undertakes to respect this supranational character and not to seek to influence the members of the High Authority…"Art. 9 ECSC Treaty — say: mark no. 3, independence; ancestor of the Commission's independence.

The key: langue vs parole (Saussure)

Why elites chose it — not only imposition

② the social 1900–1968 — critique and project

Critique of CLT: abuse of deduction (decisions taken for other reasons dressed as logic: rule → gap-filling deduction → "constructions") + individualism ignoring interdependence (urbanisation, industrialisation, organisations, global markets). Aim: save liberalism from itself — anti-Marxist and anti-laissez-faire; a harmonic, not a conflict ideology.

Innovators German (Jhering, Gierke, Ehrlich), globalizers French (Saleilles, Geny, Duguit…); USA: Holmes, Pound, Cardozo.
