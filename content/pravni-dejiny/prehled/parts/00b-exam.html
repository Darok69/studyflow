Model: Question 1 of the past exam

Sovereignty … cannot be represented … The people of England regards itself as free, but is grossly mistaken: it is free only during the election of members of parliament.Rousseau, Du contrat social (1762)
  • Asked: which French jurist developed sovereignty 200 years earlier · who bears sovereignty for Rousseau · which form of state predominated in Europe around 1762, and were there democracies · why the English are "slaves" · which element of modern democracy he attacks.
  • Answer skeleton: the author and the date → his doctrine (the general will, which cannot be represented) → the reality of 1762 → his attack on representation, i.e. on parliamentary democracy → Topic 8

The other questions of that exam (with what the model answer covered)

questionkey points of the model answer
First written law, Fertile Crescent → Topic 2Crescent around the Syrian desert, watered by the Nile, Euphrates and Tigris; hieroglyphics and cuneiform before 3000 BC. Stele of the Vultures (Eannatum of Lagash, c. 2460 BC, victory over Umma, an oath not to cross the border = "a sort of peace treaty"; now in the Louvre).
Berlin (Congo) Conference 1884–85 → Topic 13Bismarck invites 13 European states + USA; Austria-Hungary takes part, 7 states come home with no colonies. Legal results: free trade and navigation (Congo, Niger); effective occupation, Art 35 (only lands on the coast); Art 6 "blessing of civilization", suppression of the slave trade. It formalised the scramble for Africa. Extra: Congress of Berlin 1878 → Austria-Hungary may occupy Bosnia-Herzegovina; annexation in 1908.
Communists after WWI → Topic 16Hungary (Béla Kun, March–Aug 1919), Bavaria (April–May 1919). Austria: Austromarxism as a third way (Bauer, Renner, Adler, Hilferding).
Austrian constitutional development → Topics 16–19B-VG 1920 → amendments 1925 and 1929 (president stronger; Heimwehren, Mussolini as model) → March 1933 parliament "eliminates itself", emergency decrees → Feb 1934 civil war → May Constitution 1934: corporative state, one party (Vaterländische Front), Art 1 "Christian, German federal state on a corporative basis", God as source of all law → July 1934 Dollfuss murdered → Schuschnigg → Anschluss 1938.

Exam technique — always ask yourself

  • Can I name author, date, place of the extract in the first line?
  • Which doctrine is in it, and who held the opposite view?
  • What was the reality at the time (form of state, who really ruled)?
  • Which later development grows out of it? Use the arrows on the connections page.
  • Is Austria involved? The course always asks about it.
